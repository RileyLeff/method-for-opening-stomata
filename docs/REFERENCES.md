Saathoff and Welles (2021), *Gas exchange measurements in the unsteady state*,
is the dynamic-assimilation reference used for this work. Stinziano et al. (2017)
is the earlier rapid A/Ci method reference. Full publication links also appear in
the [narrative article](https://rileyleff.com/blog/forcing-plants-to-participate).
The standalone downloaded paper remains outside the release.

The capacity comparison code uses Nguyen reference means Vcmax25=84 and Jmax25=116
with reported errors 6 and 10. These constants are preserved from the original
analysis; reconfirm temperature, population, fitting conventions, and uncertainty
definitions before treating cross-study differences as population effects.
