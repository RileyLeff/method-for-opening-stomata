# Scope and limitations

- Low-CO2 conditioning encourages opening in these experiments; it does not
  guarantee maximal gsw or remove every diffusional limitation.
- Early Morella tests are sequential method-development observations, not a
  randomized methodological validation set with a matched simultaneous control.
- Ci is intercellular, not chloroplastic CO2. Apparent-Ci capacities and
  gm-dependent model capacities are not interchangeable direct measurements.
- ETR depends on instrument absorptance and PSII partition assumptions. Alternative
  electron sinks and respiration assumptions limit variable-J gm inference.
- gm remains weakly identified/prior-dominated. A dark flash alone does not measure it.
- The Q sequence is ascending, confounding elapsed run-state change with light.
  The Bayesian decline term is a sensitivity adjustment, not identified physiology.
- High/Low are elevation positions along a salinity gradient, also differing in
  habitat light. The supplied metadata contain no quantitative per-leaf salinity
  and do not establish molecular haplotype M identification.
- Phragmites is clonal; leaf IDs are not demonstrated independent genotypes.
- Cutting, bucket handling, measurement order, and environmental history can alter
  physiology. The metadata are incomplete for those histories. The model adjusts
  pooled within-day order but cannot cleanly separate all date/habitat effects.
- The primary gas contrast has three versus five leaves; fluorescence has three
  versus four. Thousands of repeated readings do not increase biological n.
- No resolved capacity difference is not evidence of biological equivalence.
- NPQ is an index; additive yields and Y(NO) have model assumptions. Unregulated
  yield is not a measured injury rate or direct leaf heat measurement.
- Fixed reference humidity is a boundary condition, not a guaranteed leaf VPD.
- The program/run association is supported by settings and log remarks, not a
  cryptographic copy of the actual instrument-resident source at acquisition.

These limitations are reasons to preserve the detailed data, rather than to
reduce the release to fitted maxima or selectively omit anomalous runs.
