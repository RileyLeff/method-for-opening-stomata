# Tier A: empirical Brownsville Phragmites results

## Scope

- Primary comparison: 3 high-forest sun leaves and 5 low-forest sun leaves.
- P02 is retained with caution; the contrast table also contains an `exclude_P02` sensitivity analysis.
- Shade leaves are retained as pilots but are not used for the high-versus-low sun comparison.
- P05, P09, and P15 remain quarantined.

## Main results

- Observed-domain maximum A: High median 11.27; low median 13.20; high-low -1.93; leaf-bootstrap 95% interval [-21.36, 12.69].
- Mean A over Q=200-2000 and Ci=400-1200: High median 5.15; low median 5.33; high-low -0.18; leaf-bootstrap 95% interval [-13.52, 14.47].
- A at Q=2000 and Ci=800: High median 5.29; low median 6.27; high-low -0.98; leaf-bootstrap 95% interval [-17.38, 17.22].
- Low-CO2-park gsw at Q=2000: High median 0.059; low median 0.078; high-low -0.019; leaf-bootstrap 95% interval [-0.054, 0.216].
- Dark-adapted Fv/Fm: High median 0.830; low median 0.822; high-low 0.008; leaf-bootstrap 95% interval [0.001, 0.046].
- NPQ at Q=2000 and Ci=800: High median 2.237; low median 3.224; high-low -0.987; leaf-bootstrap 95% interval [-1.302, -0.218].

The strongest valid sun leaf was P02 (Low), with an observed-domain maximum A of 28.38.

## Interpretation boundary

These are leaf-level descriptive contrasts. The bootstrap intervals quantify instability under resampling of the measured leaves; they do not remove day, collection, or order confounding. The observed-domain maximum may occur at elevated Ci and is not synonymous with field Amax at ambient CO2.
